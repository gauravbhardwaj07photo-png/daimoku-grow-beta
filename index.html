Garden Encyclopedia

Quick Guide

Chant Daimoku to grow your plant... consistency is the key. It takes 333 hours (approx. 1 million Daimoku) to grow the tree fully. You can customize your tree target hours in the settings.

Campaigns: A Campaign tab and target dropdown will automatically appear when there is an active campaign, allowing you to add and track Daimoku contributions.

Garden Elements

The Peepal Tree (Main Practice)

Known as the Bodhi tree under which Shakyamuni attained enlightenment. It represents your main daily practice. Grows from a tiny Seed to a Majestic Tree based on your progress towards your tree target hours.

The Lotus Flower (First Active Target)

Sprouts on the left soil. Symbolizes the principle of "simultaneity of cause and effect" and the ability to bloom pure and beautiful out of muddy waters. Grows, opens, and blooms as you complete Target 1.

The Bamboo Shoot (Second Active Target)

Sprouts on the right soil. Represents resilience, flexibility, and rapid growth—bending gracefully in the wind but never breaking. Grows tall and sprouts sections as you complete Target 2.

Red Apple Fruits (Advanced Milestones)

Grow on the outer branches of the Majestic Tree once you surpass your target hours (1 apple per 3 hours 20 mins). Symbolizes the actual proof, benefits, and fruits of your long-term practice.

Significance of Badges

In Nichiren Buddhist practice, actual proof is essential. Badges are medals of honor recognizing your consistent daily efforts, breakthroughs, and devotion.

What are they?

Honorary awards commemorating key milestones in your daily practice, determinations, and district campaign support.

How are they unlocked?

Unlocking occurs automatically when you hit specific milestones:

  • Cumulative Chanting: Accrue total chanting hours (e.g. 10h, 50h, 100h, up to the full 333h).
  • Consecutive Streaks: Chant daily without interruption to hit streak milestones (7, 30, or 100 consecutive days).
  • Gongyo Recital: Consistently complete your morning and evening Gongyo prayers.
  • Campaign Contributions: Participate in and log hours towards active SGI District Daimoku campaigns.

How to get going?

Go to the Targets tab to set your determinations, start chanting daily using the Chant stopwatch, and watch your unlocked badges in the Badges side drawer view!
